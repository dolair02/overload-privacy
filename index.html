Overload Workout Log — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Overload Workout Log ("Overload", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. Overload is designed as an offline-first progressive overload workout tracker where your training logs, routines, custom exercises, and personal records are stored locally on your device.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Workout Data: All logged exercises, sets, reps, weights, warm-up tags, setup notes, saved routines, and onboarding preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Local Account & Recovery Credentials: If you create a local account in Overload, your password and secret recovery answer/PIN are hashed locally on your device using SHA-256 encryption. We do not transmit or sell your personal workout logs or credentials to any third-party advertising networks.
  • Data Export & Deletion: You can export your workout history to CSV or JSON backups or permanently clear your local data at any time inside the Account tab of the app.

2. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All subscription transactions for Overload Pro are processed securely by Apple through StoreKit / the App Store (or Google Play on Android). We never receive or store your credit card number or billing details.

3. No Third-Party Tracking or Ads

Overload Workout Log contains no third-party advertising SDKs, does not track you across other apps or websites, and never sells user data.

You can manage and cancel your subscriptions at any time by going to your Account Settings on the App Store after purchase. Any unused portion of a free trial period will be forfeited when making a purchase.
